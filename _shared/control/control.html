CWriting: Review

A review is your evaluation of something you have read or seen, such as a film, TV show, video game or book. A review could also focus on somewhere you have been – for example, a festival, concert or restaurant – or even something that you have used, such as computer software or equipment.

Before writing

When you write a review, you need to make a judgement about something and explain your assessment of it. There are different ways to write a review, but we are going to follow this four-step plan:

1 Introduction
Hook the reader
2 Brief description
What, where, when
3 Opinion
Your views on each feature
4 Recommendation
Should people go?
CWriting: Review

Look at the following task and decide:

  • what you need to include in your review
  • who is going to read it.

You recently visited a new fast-food restaurant in your town with some friends. Your teacher has asked you to write a review of the restaurant for the school magazine.

In your review, you should give your view of the restaurant and say whether you recommend it and why.

Opposite are some comments from the friends who went with you.

Now write a review for the school magazine.

The comments may give you some ideas, and you should also use some ideas of your own.

The staff were friendly and helpful.
I was disappointed by the menu.

WRITING TIP

In any writing task, it is important to understand who your audience is. This will help you to decide if your writing should be informal, neutral or formal.
For this task:
What do you need to include?
Your views about a local fast-food restaurant: give your view of it and say whether you recommend it and why.
Who is going to read it?
People who read the school magazine (other students, teachers, parents), so the style should be neutral or formal.

CWriting: Review
Burger Delight — the complete review
1 Introduction
Do you think we need another fast-food restaurant in town? Your answer to that question will probably depend on how easily you can already access a fast-food outlet, and how much you enjoy fast food.
2 Brief description
However, whether you think we need it or not, a new fast-food venue is here. Last weekend, Burger Delight opened its doors. It is open from midday until midnight, seven days a week, and moreover, for the next month, all soft drinks are free of charge. Consequently, my friends and I decided to visit.
4 Recommendation
To sum up, I doubt if I personally would return to Burger Delight because of its location. But, if you like guaranteed quality fast food at a reasonable price, then you should visit this new restaurant.
3 Opinion
Unfortunately, we were disappointed at the inconvenient location on the outskirts of town. The nearest bus stop was considerably further away than we thought. Although the outside of the restaurant was ugly, I was pleasantly surprised by the inside, which was clean, with attractive décor. In addition, although it was busy, the staff were friendly and very helpful when we asked questions. On the other hand, I was disappointed by the menu because the choice was quite limited and boring. Nevertheless, the quality of the food was excellent and everything we tried was actually delicious. Finally, the prices were mostly very reasonable, and as a result nothing seemed overpriced.

EXAM-STYLE QUESTION

Writing, formal writing

You recently saw a television programme about fast food. Your English teacher has asked you to write a review of the programme for the school magazine.

In your review, say what you thought of the programme and what you learnt from it.

Here are some comments from other students who saw the programme:

The information about eating fast food was very surprising.
I love fast food more than ever now.

Now write a review for the school magazine.

The comments above may give you some ideas, and you should also use some ideas of your own.

Write about 120 to 160 words.

You will receive up to 6 marks for the content of your review, and up to 9 marks for the language used.

[Total: 15]

WRITING TIP

Read the instructions carefully, underlining the key words. In this task, you have been asked to write a review, so think about the kind of people who might read it. Before you start writing, spend a few minutes noting down a brief plan of what you are going to say.
Plan ideas
  1. Introduction – name of the programme; a question to hook the reader
  2. About the programme – what it showed
  3. What you thought – good and bad points (In my opinion …)
  4. What you learnt – facts that surprised you
  5. Recommendation – who should watch it and why

Readers: students and teachers → neutral / formal style.
