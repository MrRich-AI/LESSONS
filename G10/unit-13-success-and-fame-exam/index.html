Text 13.3
David Garrett
– New tour announced

1During the late 1990s and first decade of the 21st century, the great violinist David Garrett was almost permanently in the public eye. With his long hair and charming smile, and, as was fashionable at the time among young classical musicians, usually dressed in designer clothes, the German star was a favourite of the media and of many adoring fans.

2Garrett’s popularity was not just because of his appearance. Critics agree that, in terms of his technique as a violinist, he has always been a level above his contemporaries. In one stunning demonstration of his skills, he set the Guinness World Record for the World’s Fastest Violinist, playing ‘Flight of the Bumblebee’ by the Russian composer Rimsky-Korsakov.

Text 13.3 · David Garrett – New tour announced (continued)

3Born in 1980 in Aachen in Germany, Garrett revealed his musical talent at an early age, as musicians often do. At four, he picked up his older brother’s violin and started to teach himself to play. Within three years, he was attending a special music school, and at the age of ten, he performed in public with the Hamburg Philharmonic Orchestra. During his teenage years, he played many concerts and made several recordings of pieces by Mozart, Tchaikovsky and other classical composers. In 1999, at the age of 19, he moved to New York in the USA to study music at the famous Juilliard School – an experience that he claims not only improved his playing and arrangement skills, but also encouraged him to explore styles of music beyond traditional classical.

4By the early 2000s, Garrett had a busy schedule of touring and recording, which created enormous pressures on him, both psychologically and physically. Recognising the importance of protecting his health, Garrett attended daily fitness sessions at a gym, and he took up meditation with a particular focus on his breathing.

Text 13.3 · David Garrett – New tour announced (continued)

5Nowadays, Garrett’s schedule is less intensive than it was. He spends less time performing in public and more on composition, arranging and recording. In fact, his last major tour was two years ago, so the recent announcement of a series of concerts in the near future is very exciting. In interviews, Garrett has revealed that although considerably older than when he initially became famous, he hasn’t changed much in some ways. Photos and video clips show a man who is still strikingly handsome, and always very relaxed and confident in his manner, whether playing music or talking. For several years now, he has carefully avoided junk food, and he also makes sure that he sees his family on a regular basis; they provide the emotional support he needs to cope with his demanding life. It will be fascinating to see what this more mature version of Garrett is like in concert.
