SKILLS FOCUS: Writing

You will write a short review in response to a scenario. The review will be a piece of formal writing, and should take into consideration purpose and audience.

LEARNING INTENTIONS

By the end of this unit you will be able to:
  • understand and use a range of vocabulary related to the topic of food
  • understand how to identify the key features of a formal writing task, including audience, purpose, tone, register and language
  • clearly communicate information and opinions in a review
  • understand the function of linking devices and use them in your writing
  • use appropriate phrases to express opinions, agree and disagree in spoken English.

Text 3.1Click a gap to reveal the B8 answer

Things they don’t tell you about fast food

[1] Fast food may seem quite (a). . .harmless, but it has more secrets than you can imagine. The negative side of fast food is that it may make you feel unwell and cause you to become overweight. It can also seriously empty your wallet! But on the positive side, if you understand the secrets and follow our advice, you can eat more healthily, control your weight and not be out of pocket.

[2] The first secret: according to one study that included nearly 900 participants, even if you generally enjoy a healthy diet, eating even a small amount of fast food can increase the risk of feeling depressed. And anyone who frequently eats greasy burgers, nuggets and fries is at a greater risk (more than 50%) of becoming depressed than those who never touch them. However, if you reduce your fast-food habit to a couple of times per month or less, and only get your fast food from healthy outlets such as salad bars, you are less likely to feel depressed.

[3] Secret number two is that the (b). . .majority of people have no idea how many calories they are (c). . .consuming when they have a burger with fries. Recent research involved asking nearly 2000 adults, more than 300 schoolchildren and just over 1000 teenagers how many calories they thought their fast food contained. Compared with the (d). . .actual data, the research participants (e). . .underestimated the number of calories by around 200, or by about 40%. Our advice is to check the calories on the menu and try to choose something with 500 calories or less.

Text 3.1 (continued)Click a gap to reveal the B8 answer

[4] Just because something on the menu sounds healthy, that doesn’t mean it is, and this is our third secret: food that sounds healthy is sometimes worse than food that seems like it’s bad for you. As an example, one fast-food restaurant offers a thin-based pizza with plenty of vegetables and without any cheese or meat. It sounds as healthy as you can get with pizza, right? But just two slices of this contain 1300 calories, 60 g of fat and a large amount of sodium, (f). . .guaranteed to send your blood (g). . .pressure rocketing. In some fast-food outlets, even the salad options were found to be sprinkled with chemicals to keep them looking fresh. Our advice? Don’t be fooled into buying something just because it sounds healthy. Read all the information very carefully before you order.

[5] Many of us believe that buying fast food is an inexpensive option, but our fourth secret is that this is simply not true. If you’re prepared to cook at home, going to a supermarket and choosing ingredients for a meal can work out (h). . .considerably cheaper than ordering a takeaway meal.

[6] Our fifth and final secret is about that delicious barbecue taste of fire and smoke. If the food is cooked inside a restaurant, where does that taste come from? A lot of fast food is described as containing ‘natural smoke flavour’ but, believe it or not, this is commercially produced and sold to fast-food outlets to add to their menu items artificially. The only advice we can offer is (i). . .simply not to be (j). . .fooled!

[7] Hopefully now that you know these five fast-food secrets, you can make more informed choices about what you buy and eat. We hope this will make you feel healthier, be more in control of your body, and perhaps even save some money at the same time.
